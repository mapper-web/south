About this map

This is an unofficial map. It was made independently, by someone who does not attend or work for the college. It is not produced, reviewed, endorsed or maintained by the Community College of Allegheny County, and the college is not responsible for anything in it.

Do not rely on it in an emergency. Follow the posted evacuation plans and the instructions of college staff.

Nothing here has been checked against the building. Room numbers were read from CCAC’s published floor plans. Room names, stair and lift connections, links between wings and door positions are all unverified, and some are known to be wrong. Distances are estimates.

For official information, use ccac.edu or the wayfinding kiosks at the front entrances.
